Please fill in the details below for everyone staying at the apartment, including children. This is a legal requirement under Spanish law.

Why is this required? Under Spanish law (Royal Decree 933/2021), landlords are obliged to report the identity details of everyone staying at the apartment to the Spanish authorities via SES.HOSPEDAJES within 24 hours of arrival. Please submit your details at least 48 hours before arrival. Valid documents: passport or EU identity card (driving licences are not accepted).

Children under 14: must also be registered, but require fewer details — no identity document is needed. Please add them in the separate section further down.

Note on booker vs. guests: The person filling in this form (the booker) is not automatically counted as a guest. If you are also staying at the apartment, please tick the box below and include your own details as Guest 1.
